EmuVR Posters

A personal, non-commercial companion app for the VR game EmuVR.

What it does: EmuVR Posters lets me sign in with my own Pinterest account and view my own boards and pins inside EmuVR, a virtual-reality room that runs on my personal Windows PC. I pick images from my boards and display them as wall posters in my virtual room.

It is read-only. The app never creates, edits or deletes pins or boards, and it is used only by me.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

  • Data accessed: with my permission (Pinterest OAuth), the app reads my account name, my boards and the pins in them (titles and image URLs). Scopes requested: user_accounts:read, boards:read, pins:read.
  • Where data is stored: only on my own computer. Images I choose as posters are saved as local files used by EmuVR. The access token is stored locally and is never sent anywhere except to Pinterest's API.
  • No sharing, no selling: no data is sent to any server owned by this app, no analytics, no advertising, no third parties. There is no backend.
  • Other users: the app is not distributed; it is used only by its author.
  • Deleting data: disconnecting the app in EmuVR deletes the local token. Access can also be revoked at any time from Pinterest (Settings → Security → Apps). Local poster images can be deleted from the EmuVR folder.
  • Contact: please open an issue at github.com/chirola30/emuvr-posters/issues.
